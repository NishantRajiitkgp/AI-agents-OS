## Context

Carried over from `P0-6` in the bootstrap plan, which is the one open decision the whole M6
milestone waits on. It is a decision and not an implementation: nothing in this repository can
produce the answer, which is why it sits in `waiting` rather than `todo`.

The requirement it satisfies is `TRIAL-2`, and the reason that requirement is worth this much
delay is that a trial on a project chosen after the fact measures the choosing. Delivery
history is the scarce part — time-to-merge, rejection rate and defect escape rate all need a
before, and a project without one can only produce an after.

Bring this forward if `ADR-001`'s revisit trigger fires. If three or more M3 gates cannot be
validated against a CLI-shaped project, the gates are being calibrated on the wrong workload
and the choice of subject stopped being a scheduling question.

## Outcome

Open. `M0-03`'s deferral is the nearest precedent for how this should be recorded if it cannot
be answered: *not chosen* is a different fact from *no suitable project exists*, and collapsing
the two would let the trial quietly lapse instead of being abandoned on the record.
