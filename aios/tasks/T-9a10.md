## Context

Carried over from `M6-01`. This is the one task in the trial that cannot be done late: a
baseline measured after adoption is not a baseline, it is a second reading of the treated
state. Every other M6 task can slip; this one expires.

It is also the task most likely to be skipped, because it arrives at the moment when everyone
involved has already decided to adopt the thing and stopping to measure feels like delay.
`TRIAL-1` exists to make that skip visible rather than reasonable.

Without it the trial concludes nothing. The kill criteria in `T-9a4d` are comparisons, and a
comparison with one side missing is an opinion.
