## Context

Twice now, running a hook by hand has taken the write lease in the real repository under an
invented identity, and the next genuine edit was refused until the file was deleted. The first
time it was the test suite, on 2026-08-02, and the control that came out of it protects tests.
The second was a person debugging, on 2026-08-03, and nothing protects a terminal.

The two cases have one shape: the hook cannot tell "decide, and record that you decided" from
"tell me what you would decide". Everything it does is a side effect, so any exercise of it is
also a claim.

This matters more than a debugging annoyance, because the moment a control is unpleasant to
run by hand is the moment it stops being run by hand, and after that its behaviour is known
only from the two places it fires automatically.

## Outcome

Open. Raised by the incident, not by a plan, which is the only reason it is on the list.
